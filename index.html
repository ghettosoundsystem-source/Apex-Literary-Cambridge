About the Author

Darryll Lee Robert Grass

Born and raised in Cambridge, England, Darryll Lee Robert Grass writes from lived experience—spanning 1970s Cambridge childhood streets, the railways, master craftsmanship, deep genealogical archives stretching back to 16th-century Burgundy, and unwavering dedication to family.

Through Apex Literary Cambridge, his works bridge gripping historical storytelling with practical, empowering guides designed to support young people and families navigating real-world medical and life challenges.
